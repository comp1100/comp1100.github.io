# Week 13: Showcase

In week 13, COMP1100 and COMP7110 will each run a separate class showcase where everyone presents their product and gives a pitch about their project.

**WHEN:** During your course’s usual allocated studio in week 13.

**WHAT:** An informal showcase of your project. You will need:

- A one-minute pitch that helps visitors understand the customer problem, your value proposition, the MVP and the evidence supporting your important claims.
- An A2 poster that includes the team members, customer problem, value proposition and concise evidence connected to the claim it supports. This can be:
  - A single A2 printout.
  - 2 × A3 printouts.
  - 4 × A4 printouts.
- A working demonstration of the functional software MVP your team implemented.

Poster evidence may include deidentified interview findings, counts or carefully qualified percentages, MVP-testing observations, comparisons, or a relevant external source. Cite external material and do not include identifiable participant information.

**WHY:** To practise communicating technical work, share what your team accomplished and learn from other projects.

**WHO:**

- Everyone must rotate through pitching, demonstrating the MVP and answering questions.
- Everyone must visit other teams, so take turns staffing your table.

**WHO ELSE:**

- Staff from the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.
- Feel free to invite your friends.

**WHAT WE CARE ABOUT:**

- The showcase gives teams a chance to share their project with the class and see what others have done.
- We are not assessing your public-speaking performance.
- The MVP, poster PDF and supporting repository evidence remain part of the Iteration 3 submission requirements.

**TIPS:**

1. Rehearse with a timer to stay within the one-minute limit.
2. Check that the pitch, poster and demonstration make consistent, evidence-supported claims.
3. Test the MVP and prepare a reliable demonstration path.
